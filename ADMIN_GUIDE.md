# Owner guide

Visit /admin/ on the owner-private Site. First authenticated owner access binds the Sites identity when bootstrap is enabled. Disable bootstrap after binding and before broader access.

Overview counts only saved records. Lead inbox searches/filter/exports; open a reference to add notes, change stage or verify travel. Calendar shows requests, not confirmed availability. Proposal builder saves independent draft line-item versions; nothing is sent. Travel uses verified road miles and required nights: strictly >140 miles AND nights >0 means $500/night for private events only. Corporate/venue logistics stay custom.

Content & SEO shows all registry routes. Edit JSON title/body blocks and metadata, save draft/review or update eligible private-preview pages. Draft future routes remain unpublishable. Homepage/legal changes require source review. Business facts screen shows supplied/unverified claims; locked fact editing is source-controlled in this release.

Integrations exposes real connection states, notification issues and audit actions. Configure verified owner email before retrying messages. Customer receipts and automatic retries are not implemented. Team roles/MFA, media rights, general claim approvals and full revision history remain roadmap work.
