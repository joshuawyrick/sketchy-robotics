# Public launch checklist

Current release is an owner-private working preview; the master roadmap is not fully complete.

1. Owner reviews desktop/mobile, routes and all three funnels.
2. Bind owner identity, disable ALLOW_OWNER_BOOTSTRAP, test deployed non-owner/anonymous denial.
3. Supply verified public contact and legal operator; approve final privacy/terms and retention.
4. Supply approved actual paper portraits and drawing video. Concepts are not event proof.
5. Configure email sender/DNS/destination; complete receipts, delivery/retry/alerts.
6. Finish production bot controls, monitoring, backups/restore and privacy workflows.
7. Test deployed admin content/quote/lead updates and permissions; add staff roles/MFA if needed.
8. Complete schema validation, SEO crawl, Lighthouse/accessibility and original guide sourcing.
9. Connect sketchyrobotics.com, verify canonicals and hostname/slash redirects.
10. Only after explicit public-launch approval: change audience and enable PUBLIC_INDEXING_ENABLED; verify sitemap and Google/Bing ownership.

No domain, DNS or public-audience change has been made in this release.
