# Data handling

The marketing forms collect event/venue and contact details, not face photos or payment cards. Required contact authorization is separate from optional unchecked marketing email consent. Consent version/time and source path persist. No advertising or third-party analytics tracker is active. Incomplete form answers stay in active memory only.

Lead reads/updates/exports require the bound owner. Public references are opaque. CMS content is text-escaped. CSV export mitigates formula prefixes. No upload endpoint is offered. Legal pages are clearly labelled private-preview drafts.

Final legal operator/contact, retention/deletion verification workflow, backups/restore drill, compliance review and child-facing kiosk photo permissions remain public-launch requirements. The separate kiosk control/capture app is outside this marketing site.
